 # Materials

Contains the following materials:

* [ESTA Application](https://github.com/FennStatistics/Article_ESTAvalidation/tree/main/Materials/ESTA%20Application) - The final validated item list (`ESTA_final_items.csv`) plus a guide (`HOW_TO_APPLY_ESTA.docx`) for researchers who want to administer ESTA in a new study.
* [ESTA wording ethics theories](https://github.com/FennStatistics/Article_ESTAvalidation/tree/main/Materials/ESTA%20wording%20ethics%20theories) - The exact wording of the ethical theories for laypersons and shown pictures within ESTA.
* [Figures](https://github.com/FennStatistics/Article_ESTAvalidation/tree/main/Materials/Figures) - Manually crafted figures within the article.
* [Scenario Texts](https://github.com/FennStatistics/Article_ESTAvalidation/tree/main/Materials/Scenario%20Texts) - Scenario texts of four different technologies, whereby one was framed in two different ways.
